Privacy Policy: Classroom Render Farm

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Classroom Render Farm ("the farm") is a classroom tool, run by a teacher, that lets school lab computers render Blender animation frames together. This page explains what data it uses and how.

What Google data the farm can access

The farm asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It allows the farm to see, create, change and delete only the Google Drive files that the farm itself created. The farm cannot see or open any other files, email, contacts or other account data.

What the farm stores

  • Render jobs: the Blender scene file sent by the teacher, the footage and images the scene uses, and the frames rendered by the lab computers.
  • Small status files: which lab computer is rendering which frames, by computer name, and when.

These files are kept in a folder called Classroom Farm in the Google Drive account that runs the farm. They are not stored anywhere else, apart from temporary copies on the lab computers while they render, which are removed afterwards.

How the data is used

Only to render the teacher's frames and return them. The farm does not use the data for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose. It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and it does not use the data to train artificial-intelligence models.

Students and footage

Shots may include video of students filmed in class. The teacher who runs the farm is responsible for following the school's rules on student images and media consent. Footage is only stored in the farm's own Drive folder, as described above.

Keeping and deleting data

Jobs remain in the Classroom Farm folder until the teacher deletes them. The farm moves files it removes to the Drive's Trash, where Google deletes them after 30 days. To remove everything, delete the Classroom Farm folder and empty the Trash. To stop the farm accessing the Drive at all, remove "Classroom Render Farm" at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The farm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
